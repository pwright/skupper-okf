# Skupper Personas

Skupper reduces the pressure to place every communicating component on the same platform.

These personas align with the OpenShift RBAC tier model where applicable, mapping Skupper responsibilities to the three operational levels: **Cluster Administrator**, **Resource Owner** (Platform Engineer / Namespace Admin), and **Resource Consumer** (Application Developer).
