# Technology Leaders and Migration Sponsors

> Skupper can reduce the scope of a modernization project by allowing components to move separately while continuing to communicate. Teams can preserve systems that still serve their purpose and coordinate changes around business needs and operational readiness.

For this audience, the potential value is smaller change scope and independently paced migration. That value is conditional: the remaining latency, transfer cost, operational complexity, and remote failure modes must be included in the decision.
